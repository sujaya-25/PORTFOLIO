--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CRITICAL SAFETY NOTES:
   * 1N4007 Flyback Diode: Placed across the solenoid coil in reverse bias to 
     suppress back-EMF voltage spikes when the relay turns off, protecting the 
     Arduino microcontroller from inductive resets.
   * Common Ground: The external 12V power supply ground must be tied to the 
     Arduino GND to maintain equal potential reference.
